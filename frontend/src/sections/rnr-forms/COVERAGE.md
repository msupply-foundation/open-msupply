# R&R forms — behaviour coverage (C1 traceability)

Maps every behaviour of
[`spec/rnr-forms/cases/OMS-REG-REPL-07`](../../../spec/rnr-forms/cases/OMS-REG-REPL-07%20-%20Validate%20R&R%20Forms%20Creation%20and%20Management.md)

[`spec/rnr-forms/acceptance.md`](../../../spec/rnr-forms/acceptance.md)) to

vitest, the repo's test culture); interaction and real-backend criteria are the
`e2e/` Playwright tier (per

implementation) — **the vertical's deterministic suite is not yet authored**
(`/author-deterministic-tests`); until it lands, the rows marked _e2e_ are
covered by the build's live verification pass only.
